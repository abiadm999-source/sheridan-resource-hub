Redermax™ Antibacterial Wound Matrix

A fully synthetic, absorbable wound matrix with PHMB and no human or animal materials.

How it works

Synthetic matrix with PHMB

Per FDA 510(k) summaries K251582 and K261646. Bench and animal testing only.

Step 1

Placed dry on the wound

Soft, white, conformable, non-friable sheet. Perforated.

Kreate Medical schematic (SurCura Antibacterial sell sheet)
Step 2

PHMB within the matrix

In vitro, PHMB (2.5 wt%) reduces bacterial growth within the matrix for up to 7 days.

Kreate Medical schematic (SurCura Antibacterial sell sheet)
Step 3

Resorbs

Substantial resorption by Day 14; complete degradation about Day 28 (in vitro and animal data).

No clinical studies have been conducted, and no clinical benefit has been demonstrated related to the role of this antibacterial. (FDA 510(k) summary)

Evidence

What the evidence includes

No Redermax clinical cases or human outcome data on file. Evidence is limited to FDA 510(k) clearance (K251582 / K261646) based on bench and preclinical testing.
Regulatory

FDA 510(k) clearance K251582

Cleared February 2, 2026. Predicate: PuraPly Antimicrobial (K051647).

Evidence typeBench, in vitro and animal (minipig) testing; ISO 10993 biocompatibility
Clinical dataNone. No clinical studies conducted.
UpdateK261646 cleared August 14, 2026 for updated labeling; no clinical testing conducted, referenced, or relied upon.
Explore full studyDesign, results, limitations, citation
  • In vitro: at least 4-log reduction within the matrix up to 7 days (modified AATCC 100)
  • In vitro degradation: 90 to 100% mass loss at 28 days
  • Sterility assurance level 10-6
  • Does not claim to block bacteria from passing through the device (perforated)

Sources: accessdata.fda.gov K251582.pdf and K261646.pdf.

No clinical case studies available. Cases pending from Kreate Medical.

Details

Product information

What it isFully synthetic absorbable matrix
  • Electrospun composite fibers of PLGA, PDO, Poloxamer 188 and PHMB (2.5 wt%)
  • No human or animal materials
  • Sterile, single use, supplied dry in sheet form
  • FDA product code FRO; prescription use
IndicationsPer FDA 510(k) summary

Partial and full thickness wounds; pressure, venous, diabetic and chronic vascular ulcers; tunneled/undermined wounds; surgical wounds (donor sites/grafts, post-Mohs, post-laser, podiatric, wound dehiscence); trauma wounds (abrasions, lacerations, partial-thickness or second-degree burns, skin tears); draining wounds.

Contraindications and warnings: IFU not on file.
