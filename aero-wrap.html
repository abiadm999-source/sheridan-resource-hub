Sizing

What to measure

Tap a garment to see measurement points.

Flex (A6583)

Measure: Foot, ankle and largest calf circumference.

Sizing: Sizes S, M, L, XL. Height Standard (up to 6'0") or Tall.

Thigh (A6585)

Measure: Bottom of thigh and top of thigh circumference.

Sizing: Sizes S or M/L.

Full Leg (A6586)

Measure: Foot, ankle, largest calf, bottom of thigh and top of thigh.

Sizing: Below knee and above knee sizing combined.

Foot (A6587)

Measure: Foot circumference.

Sizing: Sizes S, M, L.

Arm (A6588)

Measure: Upper arm (max 50.8 cm), forearm (max 37.6 cm) and wrist (max 33 cm).

Sizing: Order the smallest size that fits both upper arm and forearm. Confirm arm chart with Sheridan before ordering.

Extension panel adds S/M 3 in (8 cm) or L/XL 5.5 in (13 cm). Size charts are on the Sun Scientific order forms; Sheridan can review measurements with your staff.
