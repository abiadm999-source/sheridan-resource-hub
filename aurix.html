Evidence

Clinical studies by level

Pragmatic RCT

DFU: 48.5% vs 30.2% healed with Aurix vs usual care.

Details, limitations, citationn=129
  • 129 patients, 28 sites; CMS Coverage with Evidence Development study
  • Limitations: open-label pragmatic design

Gude W, et al. Adv Skin Wound Care. 2019;32(9):416-426. As summarized in Nuo Body of Evidence.

Blinded RCT

DFU 7 cm² or smaller: 81.3% vs 42.1% healed (P=.036).

Details, limitations, citationn=40
  • 40 patients randomized; result shown is the per-protocol subgroup of wounds 7 cm² or smaller
  • Limitation: small sample

Driver VR, et al. Ostomy Wound Manage. 2006;52(6):68-87.

Comparative run-in

Wound area and depth decreased within 1 to 2 weeks after starting Aurix.

Details, limitations, citation46 wounds
  • 46 wounds, own-control comparison

Carter MJ, et al. Int Wound J. 2011;8:638-650.

Retrospective

Wagner grade 3 to 4 wounds: 83% healed, mean 145.2 days.

Details, limitations, citation40 wounds
  • 40 wounds, single-group retrospective

Sakata J, et al. Ostomy Wound Manage. 2012;58(4).

Case series

Observational series in mixed chronic wounds and spinal cord injury patients.

Details, limitations, citationObservational

How it works

Prepared at the point of care

Swipe →

1

Draw

Small volume of the patient's blood.

2

Spin

Aurix centrifuge separates platelet-rich plasma.

3

Separate

PRP is collected for gel preparation.

4

Activate and apply

PRP is combined with reagents to form a gel applied to the wound.

Process images from Nuo Therapeutics wound brochure.

Contraindication: Aurix uses bovine thrombin. Do not use in patients with known sensitivity to bovine materials. See full IFU.

Applications

Intended use and practice fit

IndicationsPer Nuo fact sheet

Exuding wounds including venous leg ulcers, pressure injuries and diabetic foot ulcers, and mechanically or surgically debrided wounds.

CoverageMedicare NCD 270.3
  • NCD 270.3 (CMS, April 13, 2021) covers autologous PRP for chronic non-healing diabetic wounds for 20 weeks; beyond that, MAC discretion
  • Codes: G0465 (diabetic), G0460 (non-diabetic)
  • Payment amounts per Nuo's 2026 reimbursement guide; verify for your locality
Aurix vs FastSkinNot interchangeable
  • Aurix: platelet-rich plasma gel, centrifuge system, bovine thrombin, RCT evidence
  • FastSkin: whole blood clot, no centrifuge, no bovine thrombin, small manufacturer pilot
